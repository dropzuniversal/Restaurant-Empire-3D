How it works

Drag the left side to walk — you can't walk through tables. Stand at the pass to cook (each dish uses a clean plate), carry it over and tap the customer to serve. When they finish they leave a dirty plate — tap it to collect, then walk it to the sink or you'll run out of crockery. Tap a plate on your pass to bin it. Lose too many customers and the shift is over.
